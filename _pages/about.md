I am Ruotian Chen, a second-year master's student at the School of Instrument Science and Engineering, Southeast University. I received my B.Eng. degree in Intelligent Perception Engineering from Southeast University in 2024.

My research interests lie in reinforcement learning, optimal control, and physics-aware decision making for autonomous agents that interact with the physical world. I am particularly interested in constrained/safe RL, learning-based control, model-based planning, world models, and the integration of data-driven policy learning with physical constraints. I study these problems through applications in embodied robot learning, autonomous driving, and intelligent transportation systems.
